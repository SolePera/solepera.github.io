## Abstract  
In almost 20 years, our ACM RecSys conference has evolved from a small inaugural gathering to the large and diverse community that we are today. Together, we have built RecSys, and in the process, shaped how researchers and industry practitioners at large understand and deploy recommender systems. Year after year, we
have advanced knowledge, raised new questions, and expanded the boundaries of what recommender systems research means. We have done so while nurturing the community spirit underneath it all, advancing science, but never losing sight of the people behind it. In this work, we take a stroll down memory lane across 19 edi-
tions of RecSys. Informed by the official conference programs and proceedings, we trace the evolution of the conference and reflect
on the trends and turning points that have influenced the direction of not only the field but more so the community around it. As we
do so, we highlight new directions for a community that continues to mature, while acknowledging the tensions that come with growth. The story of RecSys is still being written, especially as new generations continue to find in RecSys their academic home.
