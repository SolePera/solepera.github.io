## Abstract  
The 19th ACM Conference on Recommender Systems (RecSys 2025) was held from September 22 to 26, 2025, in Prague, Czech Republic. This edition of the conference was envisioned as a primarily in-person event, complemented by limited virtual components in exceptional circumstances. With over 1,000 registered participants from academia and industry, RecSys 2025 continued the conference series' role as a central venue for research and practice in recommender systems and personalization.
